Before and After WriteMate

Real examples of how WriteMate transforms everyday English.

⚠ Before WriteMate
Dear Mr. Johnson, I want to inform you that I cannot attend the meeting which is scheduled for tomorrow because I have a schedule conflict. I am sorry for this inconvenience. Can we reschedule to next week?
→
✦ After WriteMate (Native)
Hi David, something's come up and I won't be able to make tomorrow's meeting — sorry for the short notice. Any chance we could push it to next week? Happy to work around your schedule.
⚠ Before WriteMate
Many studies show that using social media a lot is bad for mental health of teenagers. They spend too much time on it and this makes them feel sad and lonely. This is a big problem in today's society.
→
✦ After WriteMate (Academic)
Extensive research indicates a significant correlation between excessive social media use and deteriorating mental health outcomes in adolescents. Prolonged screen engagement has been associated with heightened levels of depression and social isolation — a phenomenon of growing concern in contemporary discourse.
⚠ Before WriteMate
I am very happy to share that I finished my MBA program. It was very hard and I learned many things. I want to thank my teachers and classmates for their help. Now I am looking for job opportunities.
→
✦ After WriteMate (Professional)
Thrilled to share that I've completed my MBA — an incredibly challenging and rewarding journey. Huge thanks to my professors and cohort for the growth along the way. Now excited to bring these skills to new opportunities. Open to connecting! 🎓
